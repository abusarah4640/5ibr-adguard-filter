Stage 65E-E6 — Readiness Decision Engine

Adds a read-only, explainable decision layer over current readiness and recent
history. Statuses: not-ready, ready-for-review, ready. A READY result requires
current readiness plus a complete seven-snapshot all-ready window with no
regressions. This stage does not activate enforcement or alter approvals.

- scripts/services/readiness_decision_service.py
- web/app.py
- web/templates/dashboard.html
- tests/test_readiness_decision_service.py
- tests/test_dashboard_readiness_decision.py
